# ReachOut

ReachOut is an Android app that helps you stay in touch with the people who matter, made with ADHD brains in mind. Instead of a to-do list of everyone you "should" call, you pick your people once, and each week a spinning wheel chooses someone for you to reach out to. One name, one tap to call or text, and a little celebration when you connect.

The app was Vibe-Coded using AI, with Claude and local models, as a way to help my loved ones with ADHD.

Your contacts are only ever read, never changed, and everything stays on your phone.

## What it can do

- **Pick your people by swiping.** Swipe right to keep someone, left to pass. Choose how often each person comes up as you go.
- **Spin to choose who to contact.** A fresh wheel of up to 12 names each week. Tap Spin and the app picks one person for you.
- **Reach out in one tap.** Call, Text or Share straight from the pick, with an optional pre-filled message.
- **Check in afterwards.** "Did you connect?" Yes earns confetti and gives that person a break before they come up again.
- **Get gentle reminders.** Nudges to spin on the days and hours that suit you. A reminder can stay in your notifications until you reach out, with Call, Text and Snooze buttons.
- **See who's up from your home screen.** Widgets show the current pick, with Call and Text buttons.
- **Make it yours.** Two wheel styles, six color palettes, light and dark mode, larger text and reduced motion.

## Coming in v1.0.1

- **Reminders that stay until you reach out** (Settings › Reminders › Keep it until you reach out, on by default). Clear all skips the reminder, it can't be swiped away on the lock screen, and if it's swiped away when the phone is unlocked it comes right back. It clears when you call, text or share your pick.
- **Buttons on the reminder:** Call, Text and Snooze 1 hr (or Spin the wheel when nobody's picked yet). The lock screen shows just the reminder, without buttons, when the phone hides notification details there.
- **Flatter round wheel:** it tilts back 32° instead of 52°, and sizes itself to fit the screen.
- **About screen** (Settings › About) showing the version number.

## What's new in v0.17

This is the first release, so everything is new.

**Adding people**
- Swipe cards with Keep and Pass, and a Continue button after 3 swipes.
- A How often choice below each card: Weekly, Monthly, Few months or Surprise me (your usual range). It resets to Surprise me after every swipe.
- Import from your phone's contacts (pick several, or add everyone) or from a .vcf contacts file.
- People you pass on stay passed and don't reappear as duplicates.

**The wheel**
- Round wheel, tilted back in 3D with a top pointer, or a tall Big Wheel that rolls like a game show wheel.
- New names drawn on a weekday you choose, with a refill when the wheel empties.
- Optional Reveal picture mode, where each person you reach uncovers part of a hidden picture (designs or your own photos).
- Spin sound, vibration and a reduced-motion option.

**Your pick**
- Call, Text and Share, with built-in or custom messages, or a blank message.
- Snooze once per spin: remind me in 1 hour, or put them back in the pool.
- A "Did you connect?" check-in with confetti for Yes and a choice of what Not yet does.

**Breaks and contacts**
- A random break after you connect (1 week to 6 months by default).
- How often for each person, including a Custom range (Settings only).
- Contacts lists for In cooldown, On your list, Passed and Ignored, with Bring back, Restore, Remove several and Ignore.

**Reminders**
- Daily, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly or Yearly.
- A reminder window, with different hours on some days (such as weekends).
- Reminders are planned again after the phone restarts.

**Look and feel**
- A depth look throughout: raised cards and buttons, and recessed choice tracks.
- Six palettes, with Terracotta as the default, plus light, dark or follow phone, and three text sizes.
- Two home screen widgets: Who's up, and Who's up with Call/Text.

**Help and safety**
- A welcome tour and a searchable how-to guide for every setting.
- Demo mode with pretend contacts and time travel for trying things out.
- Backup and restore to a file, plus your phone's automatic Google backup.
- A security review covering safe file handling for backups and photos.

## Privacy and permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read contacts | To show your contacts as swipe cards. The app can never change or delete them. |
| Notifications | For reminders to spin. |
| Run at startup | To plan reminders again after your phone restarts. |

The app has no internet permission of its own. Call, Text and Share open your phone's own apps, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Requirements

Android 13 or newer.
